# CoreMark notice

Hazard3-Doom contains or works with a Hazard3-specific CoreMark benchmark port.
CoreMark is maintained by the Embedded Microprocessor Benchmark Consortium
(EEMBC):
https://github.com/eembc/coremark

The upstream CoreMark source identifies Apache-2.0 licensing, but CoreMark also
has separate benchmark acceptable-use, result-reporting, and trademark rules.
The CoreMark name is an EEMBC trademark.

Do not assume that the Apache-2.0 source license alone governs how modified
benchmark results may be represented. Before publishing a score, compare the
exact benchmark tree and modifications against the current upstream CoreMark
rules and clearly identify compiler, flags, iteration count, clock rate,
memory configuration, source revision, and any benchmark changes.

CoreMark's published contributor credits are reproduced as acknowledgements in
`../ATTRIBUTION.md`. Preserve upstream source headers and the exact upstream
LICENSE/README terms with any redistributed benchmark source.
