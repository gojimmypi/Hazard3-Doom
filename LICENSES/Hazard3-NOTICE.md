`third_party/Hazard3`.

Primary upstream project:
https://github.com/Wren6991/Hazard3

Luke Wren (`Wren6991`).

copyright/SPDX headers. The compatible Hazard3-Doom hardware integration is

https://github.com/ulx3s/Hazard3

own license files and does not apply Apache-2.0 to unrelated Hazard3-Doom
