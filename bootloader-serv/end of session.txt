There are two specific differences worth resolving before another hardware experiment.
Detail	Our v70	TinyDFU source
Runtime command	Hard-coded 27-byte ROM	Generated from actual packed primary
Status transformation	Clears both control bits	Clears both control bits
Clock after final byte	Eight enabled tail clocks, then short delay	Separate 16-count wait with EN low

The 16-count post-command wait is a concrete difference we missed. It may matter because changing the configuration-bank mode is not necessarily instantaneous.

The bigger issue is that we copied TinyDFU's command content without faithfully reproducing its entire command-delivery and restart sequence. That made v70 less decisive than we intended.

We should not declare this timing difference the root cause yet. But it is a far better engineering lead than another arbitrary delay or status-bit experiment.
